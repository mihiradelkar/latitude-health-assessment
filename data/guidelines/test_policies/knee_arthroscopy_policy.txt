COVERAGE POLICY: Knee Arthroscopy - Meniscal Tears

POLICY NUMBER: LCD-2024-0892
EFFECTIVE DATE: January 1, 2024

COVERAGE CRITERIA:

Medical Necessity Requirements:
1. Documented symptomatic meniscal tear confirmed by MRI within past 6 months
2. Pain level documented at 5/10 or greater on validated pain scale (VAS, NRS)
3. Failed conservative treatment for minimum of 6 weeks including:
   - Physical therapy (formal program, minimum 6 weeks)
   - NSAIDs or anti-inflammatory medications trial
   - Activity modification
4. Mechanical symptoms (locking, clicking, catching) or functional limitation
5. MRI findings consistent with surgical indication (Grade II-III tear)

COVERED ICD-10 CODES:
- M23.211: Derangement of medial meniscus due to old tear, right knee
- M23.311: Other meniscus derangements, right knee
- M17.11: Unilateral primary osteoarthritis, right knee

COVERED CPT CODES:
- 29881: Arthroscopy, knee, surgical; with meniscectomy
- 29880: Arthroscopy, knee, surgical; with meniscus repair

EXCLUSION CRITERIA:
1. Degenerative meniscal tears in patients over 65 without mechanical symptoms
2. Asymptomatic meniscal tears
3. Active knee infection
4. Severe osteoarthritis (Grade IV) where arthroscopy unlikely to benefit

REQUIRED DOCUMENTATION:
1. Clinical notes documenting duration and nature of symptoms
2. MRI results with radiologist interpretation
3. Documentation of physical therapy with dates and provider
4. Medication trial documentation
5. Pain scale assessment (VAS or NRS)
6. Physical examination findings
