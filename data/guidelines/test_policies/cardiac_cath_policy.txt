COVERAGE POLICY: Cardiac Catheterization

POLICY NUMBER: LCD-2024-1055
EFFECTIVE DATE: January 1, 2024

COVERAGE CRITERIA:

Medical Necessity Requirements:
1. Patient must have completed non-invasive cardiac testing (stress test, echo, or cardiac CT)
2. Abnormal findings on non-invasive testing suggesting coronary artery disease
3. Symptoms consistent with coronary artery disease:
   - Chest pain with typical anginal pattern
   - Abnormal stress test
   - Evidence of ischemia or infarction
4. Documentation of cardiovascular risk factors
5. Failed optimal medical therapy for stable angina (if applicable)
6. No contraindications to procedure (severe renal dysfunction, contrast allergy without pre-medication)

COVERED ICD-10 CODES:
- I20.9: Angina pectoris
- I25.10: Atherosclerotic heart disease
- I21.9: Acute myocardial infarction

COVERED CPT CODES:
- 93458: Cardiac catheterization, left heart

EXCLUSION CRITERIA:
1. No prior non-invasive testing
2. Normal non-invasive testing with atypical symptoms
3. Severe uncorrected coagulopathy
4. Patient refusal

REQUIRED DOCUMENTATION:
1. Clinical notes documenting symptoms
2. Results of non-invasive cardiac testing
3. Cardiovascular risk assessment
4. Medication list
5. Previous cardiac history
6. EKG results
