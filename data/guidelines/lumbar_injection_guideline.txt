COVERAGE GUIDELINE: Lumbar Epidural Steroid Injections

POLICY NUMBER: LCD-2023-0145
EFFECTIVE DATE: January 1, 2023

COVERAGE CRITERIA:

Medical Necessity Requirements:
1. Patient must have documented radicular pain (sciatica) radiating below the knee for at least 6 weeks
2. Diagnosis of herniated disc confirmed by MRI or CT scan within past 6 months
3. Failed conservative treatment including:
   - Physical therapy for minimum of 4 weeks
   - Trial of NSAIDs or other anti-inflammatory medications
   - Activity modification
4. Pain level of 5/10 or greater on validated pain scale
5. No neurological emergency (cauda equina syndrome, progressive motor weakness)

COVERED ICD-10 CODES:
- M51.16: Intervertebral disc disorders with radiculopathy, lumbar region
- M51.17: Intervertebral disc disorders with radiculopathy, lumbosacral region
- M51.26: Other intervertebral disc displacement, lumbar region
- M51.27: Other intervertebral disc displacement, lumbosacral region
- M54.16: Radiculopathy, lumbar region
- M54.17: Radiculopathy, lumbosacral region

COVERED CPT CODES:
- 62323: Injection(s), of diagnostic or therapeutic substance(s), lumbar or sacral (caudal) epidural

EXCLUSION CRITERIA:
1. Active infection at injection site
2. Coagulopathy or use of anticoagulants
3. Allergy to local anesthetics or corticosteroids
4. Previous epidural steroid injection within past 2 weeks
5. More than 3 injections in a 6-month period

REQUIRED DOCUMENTATION:
1. Clinical notes documenting duration and nature of symptoms
2. Results of MRI or CT scan showing disc herniation
3. Documentation of failed conservative treatments with dates
4. Current medication list
5. Physical examination findings including neurological assessment
6. Pain assessment score

NON-COVERED SITUATIONS:
- Epidural injections for non-specific low back pain without radiculopathy
- Prophylactic injections
- Injections performed more frequently than every 2 weeks
